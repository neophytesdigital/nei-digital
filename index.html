I · The Vow

We are not teaching a course.
We are forging an institution.

No syllabus stays unbeatable forever — tools change, employers change, the world changes. So we never sold you a syllabus. We sell the person you become: the one companies, founders, schools and clients trust to solve the problem no one else can.

"AI is simply the infrastructure. The product is people who solve messy, real-world problems faster than almost everyone else."
II · The Weekly Flame

Six rites, repeated forever.

Not modules. Not subjects. A living loop your torch must pass through, every single week of the accelerator.

RITE I

Learn

The newest developments, workflows, techniques and business cases — every week, live. No recorded videos, nothing stale.

RITE II

Build

Students build something every week. Not optional. Building is the curriculum, not an accessory to it.

RITE III

Ship

Publish it. Use it. Deploy it. Work leaves the classroom and enters the world before the week is over.

RITE IV

Measure

Time saved, money saved, people reached, errors reduced. Always measured. Never assumed.

RITE V

Reflect

Why did this work? Why didn't it? Reflection is what turns activity into judgment.

RITE VI

Teach

If a student can't explain what they built, they don't understand it yet. Every student teaches what they learned.

III · Two Standing Rituals

The Monday Beacon. The Friday Trial.

🕯 The Monday Beacon

Every Monday: what changed in the world since last week? AI, industry, hiring, tools, salaries, threats, opportunities — summarised into intelligence you can act on. Not news. A beacon.

⚔ The Friday Trial

Every Friday, one messy situation lands on the desk: an angry customer, a slashed budget, a hallucinating model, a deadline moved up. Solve it — operationally, not technically.

V · Judgment & Ruin

Decision Labs. Failure Labs.

🗡 Decision Labs

AI gives answers. Professionals make decisions. "What would you do — and why? What if the budget is cut? What if the customer complains?" This is where judgment is forged.

🩸 Failure Labs

Every month, we break what students built on purpose. Automation fails. Data corrupts. Now fix it. Employers love a graduate who has already survived a crisis — in the classroom, before the job.
